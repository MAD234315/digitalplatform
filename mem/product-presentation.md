---
name: Product presentation
description: Product-only sample styling and WhatsApp-only pricing requirements
type: constraint
---
- Product cards and product details must never display prices, discounts, or pricing information. All pricing enquiries go to the existing WhatsApp business number with the selected product name.
- Match the supplied Full Shop Flow Preview's product cards and details, without redesigning other pages, header, footer, search, filters, timer, navigation, or admin functionality.
- Use existing catalogue information only; do not invent products, descriptions, stock quantities, or sales counts. Automatically created artwork is approved for product pictures.
- Each product needs a separate working details URL that survives direct opening and refresh.
- Catalogue grid shows two product cards side by side on phones and four across on laptops.
- Every product card must have different automatically created artwork, also shown on its details page. Use a simple placeholder when artwork is unavailable.
- Sold-out products have gray, non-clickable WhatsApp enquiry buttons on cards and details pages.
- On the product details page the picture stays small and compact (a thumbnail beside the product name), never a large hero image; tapping it may open a bigger view.
